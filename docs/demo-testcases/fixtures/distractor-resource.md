# General Culinary Techniques and Recipes

A collection of classic baking and pastry methods.

## Sourdough Bread Preparation

- **Flour Selection**: High-protein unbleached bread flour produces optimal gluten structure.
- **Hydration Level**: A 75% hydration ratio yields an open, airy crumb.
- **Fermentation Time**: Bulk fermentation requires approximately 4 hours at 24 degrees Celsius.
- **Baking Temperature**: Pre-heat Dutch oven to 230 degrees Celsius before loading the dough.
